Why USB gamepads don't rumble on Android

Android passes the buttons and sticks of most USB controllers to games, but it does not pass rumble on to them. Games ask the controller to vibrate and the motors never get the message. That's why a GameSir G8, Razer Kishi or Xbox controller plugged in over USB feels dead in games that support vibration.

NeuroPad shows your games a virtual wireless controller (Xbox or PlayStation) that Android fully supports, feeds it with your real USB gamepad, and sends every rumble the game produces back to your gamepad's motors, within a few milliseconds.

What NeuroPad does

Rumble over USB

Game vibration plays on your gamepad's heavy and light motors.

No root needed

Set up with Wireless debugging on the phone (Android 11+) or a one-click PC installer. Root works too.

5 controller profiles

Appear as Xbox 360, Xbox One, Xbox Series, DualShock 4 or DualSense, with matching button prompts.

Rumble on the phone too

Optionally play game rumble on the phone's own vibration, for gamepads without motors.

Phone-vibration mirroring

For games that only vibrate the phone, that vibration is sent to your gamepad.

Remapping & diagnostics

Nintendo or custom layouts, back paddles, a live controller view and a rumble test.

Supported controllers

NeuroPad works with XInput (Xbox-mode) USB gamepads that have rumble motors. If your controller has a mode switch, set it to Xbox / XInput.

How to set it up

  1. Download the latest release: the APK for the phone, or the One-Click Installer if you have a computer nearby.
  2. Give NeuroPad system access once: pair with Wireless debugging on Android 11+, run the PC installer, or grant root.
  3. Plug in your USB gamepad, open NeuroPad and tap the power button. Games now see a controller that can rumble.

FAQ

Why does my USB controller not vibrate on Android?

Android passes the buttons of most USB gamepads to games, but not rumble. NeuroPad fixes this by showing games a virtual Xbox or PlayStation controller that Android fully supports and sending every rumble back to your USB gamepad's motors.

Does NeuroPad need root?

No. It uses Wireless debugging (Android 11+) or a one-click PC installer. Root is optional.

Does it work with my GameSir G8 / Razer Kishi?

The GameSir G8 Plus (MFi) and Razer Kishi Ultra are confirmed. Other XInput USB controllers with rumble motors should work; set the mode switch to Xbox / XInput.

My gamepad has no rumble motors. Can the phone vibrate instead?

Yes. Turn on Settings > Rumble > Rumble on the phone too.

Does it work with Bluetooth controllers?

No. NeuroPad bridges controllers connected over USB. Bluetooth controllers usually have working rumble on Android already.

Is it free and safe?

Yes. NeuroPad is open source (MIT), has no ads, needs no account and does not talk to the internet.
