## Description

Plug a USB gamepad into your Android phone and the buttons work, but the vibration never does: Android doesn't pass rumble on to most USB controllers. NeuroPad fixes that. It's a free, open-source app that shows your games a virtual Xbox or PlayStation controller and sends every rumble back to the motors of your real gamepad. No root needed.

✅ Confirmed on real hardware: GameSir G8 Plus (MFi) on a Nothing Phone (2), Android 16. The Razer Kishi Ultra works too (user report).

⬇️ Download (free): https://github.com/abdullah-amr871/NeuroPad-Virtual-GamePad-Simulation/releases
💻 Source code: https://github.com/abdullah-amr871/NeuroPad-Virtual-GamePad-Simulation

What it does
• Rumble over USB, within a few milliseconds
• 5 controller profiles: Xbox 360, Xbox One, Xbox Series, DualShock 4, DualSense
• No root: Wireless debugging (Android 11+, no PC), one-click PC installer, or root
• Phone-vibration mirroring for games that only vibrate the phone
• Button remapping (Nintendo layout, custom, back paddles)
• Live controller view, rumble test and diagnostics
• Nothing leaves your phone: the app only talks to 127.0.0.1

Works with XInput (Xbox-mode) USB gamepads that have rumble motors. If your controller has a mode switch, set it to Xbox / XInput.
